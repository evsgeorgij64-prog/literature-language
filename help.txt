====================================================================
                    LITERATURE LANGUAGE v1.0
====================================================================

Literature Language is an esoteric programming language where 
a program is written as a theatrical play. It features heroes, 
scenes, hearts (variables), and a Director (the user).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HEROES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Heroes are declared at the beginning of the script.
Director is a reserved word and represents the user/audience.

  Heroes: Player, Counter.

Rule: Exactly 2 heroes per scene. Director cannot be declared 
as a hero (Code 001).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCENES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A scene starts with [# and ends with ].
You must specify exactly two heroes and an optional scene name.

  [#Alice and Bob enter SceneName#
  Alice: "My heart is 42. Open my heart."
  Bob: "I have to keep quiet."]

Unnamed scenes cannot be called again. Named scenes can be invoked.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. REPLICAS AND ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Assign number:
  Alice: "My heart is 10."

Assign string:
  Alice: "My heart is (Hello)."

Print own heart:
  Alice: "Open my heart."

Print partner's heart:
  Alice: "Open your heart."

Silence (do nothing):
  Alice: "I have to keep quiet."

Arithmetic (add 1):
  Alice: "My heart is my heart plus 1."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. QUESTIONS AND BRANCHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Questions end with a "?". They require a Responder.

Comparison:
  Alice: "Is my heart bigger than 10?" <If yes I have to change my heart to (big). Else I have to change my heart to (small).>
  Bob: <I will answer his question.>

Read input (from Director or another hero):
  Alice: "What is Director heart?" <After I get answer I will change my heart to answer.>
  Bob: <I will answer his question.>

Branches are written in angle brackets <...>.
You can nest conditions inside branches.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TRANSITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Direct transition (goto):
  [#Enter SceneName#]

Transition from a branch:
  Alice: "Is my heart bigger than 0?" <If yes I will start RoomRight. Else I will start RoomLeft.>

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. LISTENERS (OFF-STAGE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Heroes act backstage without being on stage.
They are written inside #...#.

  #Counter: "My heart is my heart plus 1."#

Rules:
- Up to 10 heroes per listener.
- Cannot ask questions.
- Cannot open another hero's heart.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PARAMETERS (SCENES AS FUNCTIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Pass parameters like this:
  SceneName(param_name:value, param2:value)

Receive them inside the scene:
  My heart is param_name.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. CLI COMMANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  litl run <file>     Run a Literature program
  litl check <file>   Check for syntax errors
  litl cast <file>    List all heroes
  litl scenes <file>  List all named scenes
  litl new [name]     Create a new template file
  litl help           Show this manual
  litl -version       Show version
